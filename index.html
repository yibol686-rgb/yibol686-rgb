About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Life Sciences, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, with a profound interest in plant biology. My research primarily focuses on membrane trafficking, peroxisomes, and the dynamic mechanisms of mitochondrial networks in plant systems. Leveraging my interdisciplinary background in biological sciences, bioinspiration, and mechanical design, I am passionate about exploring the microscopic world within cells using molecular biology and advanced imaging technologies.
